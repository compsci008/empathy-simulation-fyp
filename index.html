What do these mean?

Energy Represents the elderly person's remaining physical stamina while completing the activity. Lower values represent greater fatigue.

Balance Represents physical stability while standing and moving. Lower values indicate greater difficulty maintaining stability and potentially greater fall risk.

Knee Pain Represents the level of physical discomfort associated with movement. Higher levels indicate greater discomfort.

Confidence Represents how confident the elderly person may feel about safely completing the activity. Supportive and appropriate caregiving decisions can help maintain confidence.

Independence Represents the elderly person's ability to participate in or complete the activity without unnecessary caregiver intervention.

About the indicators: These values are simulation variables used to demonstrate the possible consequences of different caregiving decisions. They are educational indicators within the simulation and are not clinical measurements or medical assessments.
